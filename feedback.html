Frequently Asked Questions

Standard delivery usually takes 3–5 working days after your order is confirmed. Delivery times may vary slightly depending on your location, weekends and public holidays.

Select “Track Order” in the header or footer, then enter your order number and the email used at checkout. You will see the latest package status, its most recent location and an updated delivery estimate.

Toy Haven offers card payment and Cash on Delivery at checkout. This student website uses a simulated checkout, so no real card payment is processed.

Please contact our support team as soon as possible and include your order number. Changes or cancellations can only be requested before the package has been dispatched.

Unused items may be returned within 14 days in their original packaging. If an item arrives damaged or incorrect, contact support within 48 hours of delivery so the issue can be reviewed promptly.

Select the heart icon on any product to save it. On the Wishlist page, you can organise saved items as Interested, Owned or Not Interested and add them to your cart when ready.
