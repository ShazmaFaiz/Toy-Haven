Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Toy Haven handles and how it is used.

Last updated: 21 September 2026

1. Information we collect

Information you choose to enter may include your name, email address, delivery address, demo account details, feedback, wishlist choices, cart contents and order information.

2. How information is used

Information is used to remember your shopping choices, process the simulated checkout, display order tracking, respond to feedback and improve the website experience.

3. Browser storage

This front-end website uses localStorage to keep selected information on your device. This allows your cart, wishlist and demo orders to remain available when you move between pages or revisit the website.

4. Payments and sharing

No real card payment is processed and no backend database is connected. Information entered into this demonstration is not sold or shared with advertisers or delivery companies.

5. Data security

Because information is stored in your browser, you should avoid entering real card information or sensitive personal details. If you use a shared device, clear the website data when you have finished.

6. Your choices

You can remove stored information at any time by clearing this website’s data in your browser settings. You may also unsubscribe from newsletter updates by removing the saved website data.

7. Policy updates

This policy may be updated when website features change. The latest revision date will always be shown at the top of this page.

8. Contact us

For privacy questions, visit the Support page or email hello@toyhaven.lk.
